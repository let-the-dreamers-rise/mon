THE PROBLEM

Onchain games ask you to trust them.

  • The game runs on a server. Only the items live onchain.
  • Nobody can check who really won, or who really died.
  • The attention players and watchers give a game is captured by a platform, not paid back to them.
● REC
WHAT WE BUILT

The game itself is onchain.

  • Every footstep is a transaction: positions recorded every 0.6 s by a session key, no popups.
  • Body swaps are seeded by block hashes: everyone swaps on the same block, anyone can verify.
  • Every touch, fuse and escape is proven against both bodies' positions. No server decides who died.
  • Watchers pay to torment the players. Every fee goes to whoever survives.
